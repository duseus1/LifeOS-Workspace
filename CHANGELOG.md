# LifeOS changes

## 1.1.0 — November 14, 2026

- Hid desktop-style note tabs on phone-sized screens while keeping them on desktop.
- Formatted North American contact phone numbers in capture, edit, and read views. A leading `1` displays as `+1`; other international numbers and extensions are left as entered.

## 1.0.1 — November 14, 2026

- Centered calendar weekday labels and dates in their columns.
- Selected dates now use a filled circle; today uses an outline until selected.
- Kept the sidebar background full height while moving its content below the iPhone status area.

## 1.0.0 — November 14, 2026

- Established the first development alpha and its versioning policy. The original source snapshot is also tagged `v1`.
