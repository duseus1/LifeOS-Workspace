# LifeOS
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Approved: Tauri 2, React, TypeScript, CSS, Rust, SQLite. Web-rendered desktop app; native adapters for mobile later.
## Users
Initially Daniel, then people seeking a personal, local-first information workspace.
## Product Purpose
A brain holds personal information without requiring organization. Capture immediately, find it later, open homebase.md to begin the day.
## Capabilities and Constraints
The approved LIFEOS_PLAN.md is the product specification. The supplied hand-drawn homebase establishes the section order: tasks and reminders, calendar, Quick Notes. Ordinary Markdown and local files are authoritative. Passwords are a future isolated encrypted subsystem.
## Product Principles
- Capture without organization.
- Saved information is useful immediately.
- Context comes together in editable homebases.
- No mandatory end-of-day ritual.
## Evidence on Hand
User's hand-drawn mockup in the originating conversation and approved plan. No fabricated personal records.
